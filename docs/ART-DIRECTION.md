# Safari Knights • visual brief

The supplied pictures are mood references, not approved character designs, sprites or key art. The official Scūph logo is the brand mark.

Borrow the bold ink contours, expressive poses, forest-green pennants, warm gold trim and sunset skies. Keep paper grain on posters; simplify it away on small gameplay sprites.

Start with an original frog Scūphr: a short, wide silhouette with a saddlebag of tied cleanup bags. A battered little gardening visor suggests a knight's helm. The long-handled scooper folds against the bicycle frame for the ride and snaps open on arrival. The comedy comes from treating a tiny yard like a royal commission.

First art test: draw the same knight arriving on the bike, spotting a pile and handing a clean-yard report to the owner. Check all three poses at actual gameplay size before commissioning the rest of the crew.
